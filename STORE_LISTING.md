## Full description (≤ 4000 characters)
> APE helps you take back your attention from endless short‑form video — Reels,
> Shorts, TikTok, Spotlight — with the help of Ape, a little monkey who is your
> "scroll instinct": always wired, never full. APE steps in so Ape can finally rest.
>
> Everything happens on your phone. No account, no servers, no ads, no tracking.
>
> WHAT YOU CAN DO
> • Block or add a pause to whole apps — on a schedule or a quick timer.
> • Block or add friction to short‑form FEEDS only (Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts,
>   TikTok For You, Snapchat Spotlight, Facebook Reels) — so you can still message
>   friends without falling into the feed.
> • Set a daily reel limit — scroll freely until you hit your number, then the feed
>   locks for the rest of the day. A floating counter shows where you're at.
> • Friction mode: a short "breathe" pause, then you choose to stay out or open anyway.
> • Block mode: a firm stop that turns you back.
> • Build daily habits with a streak grid and alarm‑style reminders that ring until
>   you tap them off.
> • Commit a rule to lock it in — no edits or off‑switch until it ends, for the moments
>   you know you'll want to cave.
>
> PRIVATE BY DESIGN
> APE is fully offline. It never collects, sends, or sells your data. It uses
> Accessibility access only on your device, and only to notice when a watched feed or
> app is on screen so it can step in for you.
>
> APE won't shame you. Ape just wants you to look up.
