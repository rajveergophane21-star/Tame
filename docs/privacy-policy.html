Privacy Policy — APE

Last updated: 30 June 2026

APE is an app that helps you spend less time on short-form video feeds (such as Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, TikTok, Snapchat Spotlight and Facebook Reels) and on apps you choose, using on-screen detection and gentle blocking or friction. This policy explains what APE does and does not do with your information.

The short version: APE has no account, no servers, and no ads. It does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data. Everything stays on your phone.

Information we collect

None. APE does not collect or send any personal information to us or to any third party. There is no sign-up, no login, no analytics or tracking SDKs, and no advertising. We have no servers that receive your data.

Information stored on your device

Your settings — the rules, app/feed selections, daily limits, habits, reminder times, and counts — are stored only in APE's private storage on your own device. This data never leaves your phone through APE. Uninstalling the app removes it.

Because APE allows Android's standard backup, Android may include these local settings in your own device backup to your own Google account (Android Auto Backup). That backup is controlled by you and Android, not by APE, and we never have access to it. You can disable backups in your device settings.

Accessibility access

APE uses Android's Accessibility Service so it can recognise when a short-form video feed, or an app you chose to limit, is on the screen — and then act on the rules you set (show a stop screen, add a short pause, or return to the previous screen).

  • Screen content read through accessibility is used only on your device, in the moment, to detect feeds and the apps you selected.
  • It is never recorded, stored long-term, transmitted off your device, or shared with anyone.
  • APE does not read or collect passwords, messages, or page contents for any purpose other than recognising the feeds/apps you asked it to manage.

Permissions APE uses, and why

  • Accessibility service — detect the chosen feeds/apps on screen and apply your blocking/friction rules.
  • Display over other apps — show the stop screen and the floating reel counter on top of other apps.
  • Alarms & reminders (exact alarm) — make habit reminders ring at the time you set.
  • Full-screen notifications — show a habit reminder over the lock screen.
  • Notifications — deliver reminders and warn you if blocking gets switched off.
  • Vibrate — vibrate for reminders.
  • Run after restart — re-schedule your reminders after the phone reboots.
  • See installed apps — list your launchable apps so you can pick which ones to limit. This list is shown to you on-device only and is not collected.

Data sharing and selling

APE does not share or sell any data, because it does not collect any.

Children

APE is a general-purpose wellbeing tool and is not directed at children under 13. It does not knowingly collect any information from anyone.
